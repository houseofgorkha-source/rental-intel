## Seed Initial Content

Goal:
Ensure RentalIntel feels useful from day one by populating the platform with a curated set of well-known properties.

Tasks:

- Research 50–100 well-known residential properties in Bangalore.
- Create original property summaries.
- Identify common themes from publicly available reviews (pros, cons, recurring issues).
- Write completely original sample reviews inspired by overall sentiment, not copied or closely paraphrased from individual reviews.
- Create realistic rating distributions and review counts.
- Add amenities and basic property information.
- Replace placeholder/mock properties with these curated entries where appropriate.

Notes:

- Do not copy review text from Google or other platforms.
- Do not use copyrighted property photos without permission.
- Prefer user-contributed photos in the long term.
- This is launch content to make the platform useful until genuine tenant reviews grow organically.
